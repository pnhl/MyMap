# MyMap — Amazon Appstore listing (English U.S.)

## Display title

MyMap – Journeys, Maps & Memories

## Short description

Keep a record of your journeys, save photo memories on the map, and stay connected with friends. Explore 2D and 3D maps, plan routes, check weather ahead, and manage your location sharing in one place.

## Long description

Make your everyday journeys easier to explore and remember with MyMap.

YOUR JOURNEYS, ON THE MAP
Start journey recording to keep a location history on your device. View your daily and monthly timeline, review places you have visited, and see your recorded distance. Highlight traveled routes to discover more of the places around you.

MEMORIES WITH A PLACE
Save photos and notes with a location, then revisit them on the map. Keep the moments that matter connected to where they happened.

MAPS AND ROUTE PLANNING
Choose map styles to suit your trip, including OpenFreeMap with 3D buildings where data is available. Get directions for driving, motorcycling, cycling, or walking. View upcoming turns and weather ahead on your route. Set preferences to avoid ferries, highways, or unpaved roads where the routing service supports them.

STAY CONNECTED
Find friends by username, phone number, or QR code. Use location sharing to stay connected, and manage sharing through the app's privacy controls.

TRAVEL SETTINGS THAT FIT YOU
Set a personal speed reminder and enable sound or vibration alerts. Road speed-limit alerts are available where supported map data provides a limit. Use portrait or landscape layouts, choose your language, and manage privacy and data export in Settings.

SOS TOOLS
Set up trusted contacts and open the SOS screen to obtain your current location automatically when location access is available. On compatible devices with calling support, MyMap can open the dialer with the configured emergency number ready for you to confirm the call.

AVAILABILITY
Location recording requires a compatible device or location source and the necessary permissions. Background recording may be affected by device power settings. Online maps, routing, weather, and friend features require an internet connection. Always follow local road signs; map information may be incomplete. SOS tools do not guarantee an emergency response.

MyMap contains ads. Some online features require an account.
